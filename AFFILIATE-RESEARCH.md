## Top 5 easiest wins (Judy can join today)

1. **Magic of I** - open application, 15% commission, 15% off for you, a gifting bundle, PayPal or store credit payouts, and no stated follower minimum. Best fit for a small audience. Apply at https://magicofi.com/pages/affiliate
2. **Mountain Rose Herbs** - no follower minimum, 10% commission, a well-known trusted brand, and an in-house program with simple signup at https://mountainroseherbs.com/affiliates/ ($50 payout threshold).
3. **Ora Cacao (ceremonial-cacao.com)** - the highest commission of the group (15-20%). It is a ceremonial cacao brand that fits the audience. The catch is that you order first and get 5 referral purchases before the affiliate tier unlocks. The starter referral tier works immediately. https://ceremonial-cacao.com/pages/reciprocity-program
4. **Goddess Provisions** - the application form is open (https://www.goddessprovisions.com/pages/affiliates), the brand is aligned with the audience, and it also offers gifting and discount codes. Commission is not stated.
5. **The Ohm Store** (alternative: **Halfmoon**) - fill in the form at https://www.theohmstore.co/pages/affiliates. No stated minimums, and it is an open-approval program. Halfmoon (UpPromote, about 10%) is another simple form signup.

Honorable mentions: Classic Variable (25% store credit, direct email), Shamans Market (simple in-house), and Keith's Cacao (Affiliatly portal). DharmaCrafts needs 2k+ followers on the influencer track, so wait until Judy has more reach. House of Intuition is on FlexOffers but pays only about 2.4%. Brentwood Home is on Impact but is a poor niche fit.
